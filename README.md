# The Ordinary Universe

**Extraordinary stories. Entirely ordinary people.**

A shared comic universe of people whose seemingly extraordinary superhero names are literal descriptions of entirely normal lives. No superpowers. No supernatural events. No self-aware winking at the audience. Just spectacularly serious storytelling about trivial British problems.

**Status:** Creative development. **Initial format:** Short illustrated comic strips, primarily four panels. **Planned first publisher:** [DaveHub](https://davehub.co.uk/).

## The central rule

The characters are **completely ordinary people**, not incompetent superheroes or people with amusingly weak superpowers. The world itself is mundane. Only the framing is epic.

## What is in the repository?

This repository is the **source of truth for story, scripts, character references, art and publishing metadata**. DaveHub is a separate project that will consume finished, approved releases. Nothing in this initial scaffold is represented as published artwork or a live integration.

## Public repository and rights

Public visibility is not an open licence. See [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md). Third-party superhero names are creative parody references and working titles, not an assertion of association, endorsement or ownership. Perform an IP/design review before public promotion and avoid copied costumes, logos or protected artwork.
