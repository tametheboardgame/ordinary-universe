# The Justin League

**Motto:** *No powers. Just Justins.*  
**Membership criterion:** Must be named Justin.  
**Headquarters:** The Hall of Justins, a pub function room rented on alternate Thursdays.  
**Status:** Ensemble concept agreed; individual appearances unapproved.

## Seven Justins

| Working label | Personality | Distinguishing everyday wardrobe / prop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Justin from Accounts | Organiser; fears over-spending | Shirt, tie, clipboard |
| Hi-Vis Justin | Practical; brings tools | High-visibility vest |
| Gym Justin | Takes preparation very seriously | Sportswear and water bottle |
| Laptop Justin | Spreadsheets are the solution | Casual shirt and laptop |
| Dad Justin | Reliable, but must leave early | Everyday jacket and shopping bag |
| Quiet Justin | Speaks rarely; often right | Understated knitwear |
| Other Justin | Resents being the 'other' Justin | Flannel shirt or denim jacket |

These are **working design prompts**, not cast portraits. Never visually merge two Justins or imply identical faces. Team cohesion comes from the name, not costume.

## Group dynamics

Seven people often reply to a message intended for one. Justin from Accounts keeps minutes nobody reads. The chat is muted by most. Who is 'the original Justin' is endlessly contested. The Hall of Justins is frequently double-booked.
