# The Dark Night

**Civilian identity:** Gary Knight  
**Literal basis:** The dark hours of the night, during which Gary is often awake.  
**Tagline:** *When darkness falls, he's usually still up.*  
**Status:** Character concept agreed; appearance not locked.

## Character essence

Methodical, sleep-deprived and convinced domestic vigilance is an important calling. Checks locks and windows too frequently. His elaborate moral monologues are internal; outwardly he mostly mutters to himself.

## Recurring elements

- Dressing gown and slippers; phone torch when investigating the house.
- Repeated expeditions to inspect the already-locked back door.
- Gotham, Nottinghamshire presented as a brooding metropolis.
- Thinks Robin is a longstanding partner, despite barely knowing him.

## Story relationships

His differences with Man of Carbon concern mundane practical choices, especially rubbish collection. Robin would prefer a normal neighbourly relationship.
