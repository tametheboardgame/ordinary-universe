# Character directory

Every character is an ordinary person, not a disguised powered hero. A name is the entry joke; the personality must carry the recurring stories.

## Leads
- [Man of Carbon / Martin Clarke](man-of-carbon.md) — made of carbon, just like everyone else.
- [The Dark Night / Gary Knight](the-dark-night.md) — awake after dark.
- [The Justin League](the-justin-league.md) — seven Justins and no other admissions.

## Other recurring characters (working profiles)
- **Wonder Woman / Wendy Palmer:** Wonders about things and asks inconveniently sensible questions.
- **The Flash / Phil Marsh:** Photographer, sometimes leaves his flash switched off.
- **The Green Lanyard / Graham Finch:** Workplace access-card holder.
- **Aqua Man / Alan Waters:** Enthusiastic water drinker and swimmer.
- **Cyber Man / Simon Patel:** IT specialist confronted by vague support requests.
- **Robin / Robin Chapman:** Robin by name; the Dark Night's alleged partner.

## Antagonists
The Joker (jokes), the Riddler (pub quiz), Two-Face (two-faced colleague), Mr Freeze (office temperature), Poison Ivy (Ivy's unfortunate barbecue), Catwoman (cat owner), the Penguin (dinner jacket), Les Luthor (parking dispute), Doomsday (Monday).

**Development rule:** Before artwork, each lead needs an approved full-body turnaround, two signature facial expressions, ordinary wardrobe notes, height/proportion guide and relevant recurring props. Never use an existing superhero actor or costume as a direct image reference.
