# Man of Carbon

**Working civilian name:** Martin Clarke  
**Literal basis:** Carbon-based human life.  
**Tagline:** *He is one of us. Literally.*  
**Status:** **Appearance and model sheet approved by creator (23 October 2026).** Martin Clarke remains the working civilian name pending separate confirmation. High-resolution reference artwork generated and approved in conversation; repository binary upload still pending.

## Character essence

Well-meaning, grandiose without being cruel, Martin assumes a normal explanation must have an exceptional hidden meaning. When a GP tells him he is carbon-based, Martin assumes destiny is calling. The doctor means everyone is.

## Recurring elements

- Calls walking, digestion or getting tired 'my abilities'.
- Describes his garden shed as the Fortress of Solitude.
- Drives ordinary household disagreements towards philosophical confrontation.
- Looks like someone one might encounter in a supermarket; no cape or recognisable existing superhero suit.

## Story relationships

The Dark Night is his sometimes ally and sometimes rival in utterly insignificant matters. Les Luthor disagrees with him about parking. Wonder Woman's questions occasionally puncture Martin's certainty.

## Approved visual design (23 October 2026)

- Consistent middle-aged British man with slightly messy medium-brown hair, light stubble and dark rectangular glasses.
- Medium ordinary build, natural slightly slouched posture when relaxed, more upright when taking his invented destiny seriously.
- Red-and-charcoal checked overshirt with rolled sleeves, heather-grey crewneck tee, washed dark jeans, practical dark trainers with light soles.
- Expression sheet: neutral, startled realisation, solemn resolve and faint embarrassed disappointment.
- Four-angle turnaround: front, three-quarter, side and back; dramatic portrait as the lighting reference.
- Unremarkable belongings: a garden-shed key, shopping bag and GP appointment slip.
- **No superhero costume or cape.** Heroism is entirely a camera/lighting treatment.
