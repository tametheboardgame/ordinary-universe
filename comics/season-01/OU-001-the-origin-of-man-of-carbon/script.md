# OU-001 — The Origin of Man of Carbon

**Status:** scripted / unillustrated  
**Cast:** Martin Clarke (Man of Carbon), GP  
**Location:** An ordinary GP consulting room  
**Length:** 4 panels  
**Tone:** Dead serious medical revelation, wholly unremarkable explanation

## Panel 1 — The revelation

**Art:** Low-angle view of an ordinary GP at a desk, shot like a shadowy government scientist. Bland anatomical poster. Martin sits in a cheap plastic chair.

**GP:** "Martin, there's something you should know about your body."

## Panel 2 — The shock

**Art:** Extreme close-up of Martin's eyes, excessively dramatic golden edge lighting.

**GP (off-panel):** "It's carbon-based."

## Panel 3 — Destiny

**Art:** Martin slowly stands, coat over shoulder as if a mythic hero is being born. GP turns back to a computer.

**MARTIN:** "So... I'm different?"

## Panel 4 — Reality

**Art:** Flat fluorescent wide shot showing ordinary surgery, with waiting patients glimpsed beyond the door.

**GP:** "No. That's how people work."

**Small caption:** *MAN OF CARBON WILL RETURN.*

## Editorial/continuity

No special carbon testing or supernatural effects. The GP is not a villain. The punchline is a completely normal truth delivered without drama. Keep the title treatment outside panel 4.

**Accessibility transcript:** Panel 1, GP tells Martin there is something he should know about his body. Panel 2, the GP says it is carbon-based. Panel 3, Martin asks whether that makes him different. Panel 4, the GP replies that it is how people work.
