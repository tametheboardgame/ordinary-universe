# OU-006 — The Call to Justin

**Status:** scripted / unillustrated / awaiting storyboard approval
**Cast:** All seven Justins; pub employee
**Locations:** Ordinary British homes/workplaces; pub exterior and function room
**Format:** One double-page spread, 10 numbered panels (left 1–5; right 6–10)
**Style:** A — Cinematic Graphic-Novel
**Tone:** Epic team assembly to perform an act of perfectly normal helpfulness

## LEFT PAGE — The summons

### Panel 1 — A call goes out
**Shape:** Wide establishing panel.
**Art:** Close-up of a phone glowing in a dim office. Email subject clearly reads: "URGENT: HELP NEEDED THURSDAY". Addressee is simply "Justin".
**Caption:** "When the call went out..."

### Panel 2 — Justin from Accounts
**Shape:** Medium character panel.
**Art:** Justin from Accounts looks up from a spreadsheet, adjusting his glasses. His tie and clipboard are normal, not a uniform.
**JUSTIN FROM ACCOUNTS:** "Right. We'd better get organised."

### Panel 3 — The preparations
**Shape:** Wide montage panel with clearly separated vignettes.
**Art:** Gym Justin puts down a dumbbell; Dad Justin checks his watch; Laptop Justin closes a laptop. They are clearly distinct men.
**Dialogue:** None.

### Panel 4 — Others answer
**Shape:** Three-vignette panel.
**Art:** Hi-Vis Justin takes off his safety gloves; Quiet Justin pockets his phone; Other Justin stares at his own screen.
**OTHER JUSTIN:** "Which Justin?"

### Panel 5 — One name, seven men
**Shape:** Tall dramatic panel.
**Art:** Ordinary shoes step off seven different pavements in rainy evening light. No impossible speed or superpowers.
**Caption:** "...seven answered."

## RIGHT PAGE — The League assembles

### Panel 6 — The line-up
**Shape:** Biggest panel on right page.
**Art:** All seven distinguishable Justins stand outside a normal pub under dramatic cloud-break lighting, wearing everyday clothing. A mundane "Quiz Tonight" sign is visible.
**Dialogue:** None.

### Panel 7 — The challenge
**Shape:** Medium panel.
**Art:** Justin from Accounts stands earnestly at the door and speaks to a pub employee.
**JUSTIN FROM ACCOUNTS:** "We're here. What's the emergency?"

### Panel 8 — The answer approaches
**Shape:** Narrow quiet panel.
**Art:** The employee gestures down a hall towards the function room.
**PUB EMPLOYEE:** "Oh, brilliant. It's in here."

### Panel 9 — Their destiny
**Shape:** Wide reveal panel.
**Art:** Flat fluorescent function room. A stack of folded chairs, a bin bag and quiz-night sign-up sheet. The employee gestures to the chairs.
**PUB EMPLOYEE:** "Can you set these up before the quiz?"

### Panel 10 — The League responds
**Shape:** Wide understated closing panel.
**Art:** All seven Justins are quietly unfolding chairs. Hi-Vis Justin has already finished two. The employee gets on with normal pub work.
**HI-VIS JUSTIN:** "Yeah, course."

## Composition and continuity

- No actual super-abilities or joke costumes; everyone is simply being helpful.

## Accessibility transcript

1. An urgent email addressed to Justin requests help on Thursday.
2. Justin from Accounts starts organising.
3. Gym Justin, Dad Justin and Laptop Justin notice the same call.
4. Hi-Vis Justin, Quiet Justin and Other Justin respond; Other Justin wonders which Justin was intended.
5. Seven people head out through ordinary rainy streets.
6. All seven assemble heroically outside a pub advertising its quiz night.
7. Justin from Accounts asks the pub employee what the emergency is.
8. The employee leads the way into the function room.
9. The request is to arrange folding chairs before a pub quiz.
10. All seven calmly set up the chairs and Hi-Vis Justin agrees.
