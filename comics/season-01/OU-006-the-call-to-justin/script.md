# OU-006 — The Call to Justin

**Status:** scripted / unillustrated  
**Cast:** All seven Justins; pub employee  
**Locations:** Everyday homes/workplaces; outside and inside the pub  
**Length:** 4 panels  
**Tone:** Epic team assembly for an ordinary bit of helpfulness

## Panel 1 — The summons

**Art:** A phone dominates the frame against exaggerated storm-lit darkness. The notification itself is completely mundane.

**On-screen email:** "TO: Justin(s)" / "SUBJECT: URGENT: HELP NEEDED THURSDAY"

**Caption:** "When the call went out, they all answered."

## Panel 2 — The awakening

**Art:** Seven small vignettes within the panel: office, sofa, gym, hi-vis shift, shopping, laptop, making tea. Each Justin notices the same alert.

**Dialogue:** None.

## Panel 3 — The assembly

**Art:** Seven ordinary blokes in a dramatic low-angle line-up outside a pub; headlights and rainy weather produce cinematic backlighting. Their clothes remain absolutely normal.

**JUSTIN FROM ACCOUNTS:** "We're here. What's the emergency?"

## Panel 4 — The mission

**Art:** Flat fluorescent lighting in a function room. An employee points towards a large stack of folding chairs.

**PUB EMPLOYEE:** "Brilliant. Can you get these set up before the quiz?"

**HI-VIS JUSTIN:** "Yeah, course."

## Editorial/continuity

All seven faces must be distinguishable. Panel 2 may be simplified to three clear foreground Justins and four visible phone alerts if legibility suffers. The men have done a kind thing; their superhero presentation is disproportionate, not their behaviour.

**Accessibility transcript:** Seven men named Justin receive an urgent message asking for help. They travel from everyday routines and assemble heroically outside a pub. Their mission is to set up chairs for a quiz. Hi-Vis Justin agrees.
