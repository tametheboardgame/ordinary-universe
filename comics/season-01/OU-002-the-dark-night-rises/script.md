# OU-002 — The Dark Night Rises

**Status:** scripted / unillustrated / awaiting storyboard approval
**Cast:** Gary Knight (The Dark Night)
**Location:** Gary's ordinary semi-detached house, overnight
**Format:** One double-page spread, 10 numbered panels (left 1–5; right 6–10)
**Style:** A — Cinematic Graphic-Novel
**Tone:** A solemn domestic security patrol with no actual threat

## LEFT PAGE — The call of the night

### Panel 1 — Gotham sleeps
**Shape:** Wide cinematic opening panel.
**Art:** Quiet rooftops and a suburban street, damp tarmac under a streetlamp. Nothing remotely sinister is happening.
**Caption:** "Gotham. 2:43 AM."

### Panel 2 — One man remains
**Shape:** Small panel.
**Art:** Ordinary digital bedside clock: 02:43. A glass of water sits beside it.
**Dialogue:** None.

### Panel 3 — The vigil
**Shape:** Large moody portrait panel.
**Art:** Gary lies awake, eyes open in gloom. His partner sleeps off-panel; no heroic outfit.
**GARY (thought):** "The back door."

### Panel 4 — The burden
**Shape:** Tight close-up.
**Art:** Gary presses his fingers to his tired face.
**GARY (thought):** "Did I lock it?"

### Panel 5 — The preparation
**Shape:** Low-angle detail panel.
**Art:** Slippers touch the bedroom floor. Gary lifts his phone to turn on its torch.
**Caption:** "There could be no rest."

## RIGHT PAGE — The descent

### Panel 6 — Into the dark
**Shape:** Tall atmospheric panel.
**Art:** Gary creeps down his own staircase in a dressing gown with the phone torch; long shadows create theatrical danger.
**Dialogue:** None.

### Panel 7 — The watcher
**Shape:** Wide narrow panel.
**Art:** In the kitchen doorway, a neighbour's cat watches through the window. Gary freezes in the beam of his phone.
**Dialogue:** None.

### Panel 8 — The final test
**Shape:** Large low-angle panel.
**Art:** Extreme heroic framing of Gary's hand gripping an ordinary back-door handle.
**Caption:** "Every lock holds a secret."

### Panel 9 — Truth
**Shape:** Close-up.
**Art:** The door handle does not move. It is securely locked.
**GARY:** "Yep."

### Panel 10 — Vigil without end
**Shape:** Wide quiet closing panel.
**Art:** Gary stands beneath the switched-on kitchen light, phone in hand, profoundly tired. A normal kettle and recycling bin are in view.
**Caption:** "Third time tonight."

## Composition and continuity

- No crime, threats or supernatural presence; the cat is simply a cat.
- Reading order 1→10, page split 1–5 and 6–10; protected centre gutter.
- Gary remains in ordinary nightwear throughout. Huge dramatic lighting contrast ends with flat kitchen light.
- Script revision is not a finished layout or art approval.

## Accessibility transcript

1. A quiet suburban street is captioned "Gotham. 2:43 AM."
2. A bedside clock reads 02:43.
3. Gary lies awake, thinking about the back door.
4. He wonders whether he locked it.
5. He pulls on slippers and takes his phone torch, feeling duty-bound to investigate.
6. Gary walks down his unremarkable staircase.
7. A cat looks in through the kitchen window.
8. Gary reaches for the back-door handle.
9. The locked handle confirms the door is secure; Gary says "Yep."
10. Gary stands exhausted in the kitchen. A caption reveals this is his third check of the night.
