# OU-002 — The Dark Night Rises

**Status:** scripted / unillustrated  
**Cast:** Gary Knight (The Dark Night); partner unseen  
**Location:** Gary's semi-detached home, overnight  
**Length:** 4 panels  
**Tone:** Batman-level vigilance over an already locked door

## Panel 1 — City in peril

**Art:** Brooding view of quiet suburban rooftops. Streetlamp and cloud. No criminal activity.

**Caption:** "2:43 AM. The city sleeps. One man remains vigilant."

## Panel 2 — The mission

**Art:** Gary sits exhausted at the edge of the bed, in a dressing gown, staring at darkness.

**GARY (thought):** "Did I lock the back door?"

## Panel 3 — The descent

**Art:** Menacing silhouette of Gary creeping down an ordinary staircase, using his phone torch. His shadow looks disproportionately heroic.

**Caption:** "He ventures once more into the unknown."

## Panel 4 — The resolution

**Art:** Kitchen light is switched on. Gary tests the clearly locked back door, mug in his other hand.

**GARY:** "Yep."

**Small caption:** "Third time tonight."

## Editorial/continuity

No crime and no costume. Humour lives in scale, lighting and measured pacing. Sound design in any animated adaptation would treat turning a door handle like a seismic event.

**Accessibility transcript:** At 2:43 am Gary cannot remember whether he locked his back door. He goes downstairs with a phone torch to check it. The door is locked. It is his third check of the night.
