# Double-spread layout guide v0.1

**Decision:** 23 October 2026, approved for The Ordinary Universe.
**General format:** One two-page landscape comic spread, **8–12 panels**, with **10 panels as the default target**.
**House art:** Style A, cinematic graphic-novel.

## Story rhythm (10-panel example)

| Panels | Beat | What the art should do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Establishment | Treat a GP surgery, house or pub like a major cinematic location |
| 3–4 | Ordinary problem | Show realistic detail and allow anticipation to grow |
| 5–6 | Overreaction | Give a solemn close-up, a pause or an oversized panel |
| 7–8 | Mythic declaration | Visually stage an utterly normal action as destiny |
| 9 | Mundane reversal | Expose the obvious truth with an understated line |
| 10 | Quiet consequence | Land a small human reaction or silent afterbeat |

The rhythm is flexible. **Do not force a mechanical 5×2 grid:** panel sizes and crops can vary and a wide panel may carry a beat. Do not let complex flashbacks or panels crossing the gutter confuse reading order.

## Composition and lettering

1. Design the spread as **left and right facing pages**, conceived together.
2. Maintain a **central gutter safe zone** without faces, dialogue, panel numbers, punchlines or essential information.
3. Label each panel with an immutable reading-order number, starting on the left page and proceeding to the right. The storyboard should specify page side and rough proportion.
4. Reserve text-safe areas early. Lettering must be readable at the intended desktop size and in an individually displayed mobile panel.
5. Use contrast: one dramatic wide establishing image, tighter human shots, occasional silent panels and an unglamorous final view.
6. Panel artwork must remain consistent with the locked character model sheets, even across extreme camera angles.
7. Keep narrative text sparse: normal short dialogue, occasional self-important caption, no lengthy word balloons obscuring the joke.

## Master and web deliverables

- Editable/lossless **clean master** of the complete spread.
- Separate **lettered master** of the complete spread.
- One optimised **spread export** for large landscape displays, retaining artwork quality and dialogue legibility.
- Independently cropped **panel-01, panel-02, ...** web images, with bubbles and captions safely preserved, for mobile.
- Canonical panel order and optional left/right page assignment in the published metadata.
- Each panel has alt text and a transcript; the full issue transcript is readable HTML.
- Cover/thumbnail and asset provenance/rights records.

**Exact dimensions, gutter width and output resolutions are deliberately not locked yet.** Test them with the OU-001 storyboard and DaveHub reader design; do not impose physical-print assumptions on a web-first comic.

## DaveHub behaviour

- Wide display: present the approved composed spread, with access to a larger zoomed view.
- Narrow/portrait display: present individually optimised panels in canonical story order.
- Never rely on shrinking an entire 10-panel spread to fit a phone.
- Preserve keyboard and screen-reader navigation, alt text and transcripts.

## Special editions

A labelled special such as **Justin Snyder's Justin League** may add redundant panels or an additional spread, using heightened Style A/C treatment. Do not alter the ordinary truth, the narrative outcome or the original issue permalink.
