# Creative and technical decision log

## 2026-10-22 — Core premise

**Accepted:** A parody superhero universe of completely ordinary people, with epic, completely earnest cinematic treatment. No actual powers or fantastical events.

## 2026-10-22 — The Justin League

**Accepted:** The organisation is exclusively ordinary men named Justin. They are separate from the other recurring heroes, although crossover appearances are possible.

## 2026-10-22 — Initial publishing medium

**Accepted:** Short comic strips, normally four panels, initially intended for DaveHub.

## 2026-10-22 — Repository separation

**Accepted:** Ordinary Universe creative source is kept in its own public GitHub repository, independent of the DaveHub website repository. Publishing integration will be implemented later.

## 2026-10-23 — Ordinary Universe main illustration style

**Accepted:** The **A: Cinematic Graphic-Novel** option from the three-style Man of Carbon comparison is the default visual language for all ordinary-universe comic strips. Detailed, drawn cinematic realism; brooding atmosphere; normal British people; mundane action. Style B not selected for the house style. Refer to `artwork/style-guide.md`.

**Not yet accepted:** Any specific character face, costume/clothes variant, approved final-panel artwork, lettering master or exact colours.

## 2026-10-23 — Justin Snyder's Justin League (special recut)

**Accepted:** A deliberately overlong, dark, operatic alternate version of **The Justin League**, titled **Justin Snyder's Justin League**. It reuses fundamentally the same ordinary events and ending, introduces extra lingering beats and portentous captions, and reorders certain panels without substantively changing what happens. Stylistic exception: hyper-stylised A, potentially some of C's grain and texture. The tonal joke is a revolutionary 'director's cut' that changes almost nothing.

**Story status:** Approved concept only. No final comic script or artwork yet. See `bible/justin-snyders-justin-league.md`.

## 2026-10-23 — Man of Carbon character appearance approved

**Accepted:** Martin Clarke's first canonical visual design, in the locked cinematic Style A house style. The approved sheet presents front/three-quarter/side/back turnaround, four expressions, ordinary props and a dramatic lighting portrait. Signature look: tousled brown hair, stubble, rectangular dark glasses, red-and-charcoal checked shirt over grey tee, jeans and trainers. No cape or powers.
